ABOUT THE VENUE

What EventHub is

EventHub is a small community board for local happenings — gigs, workshops, art shows, meetups, whatever's on. Anyone can look through what's listed without signing up. Create a free account when you want to post your own event or keep track of the ones you've shared.

How it works

  • Browse events by category, or sort by date — no account needed.
  • Register to post your own event: title, description, location, date, price, and organizer.
  • Only you can edit or delete the events you've posted.
  • Each event page pulls in the current weather for its location.
